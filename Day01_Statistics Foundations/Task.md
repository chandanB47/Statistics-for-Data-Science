## Task 6 — Think Like an Analyst

A manager says:

> “The selected customers have an average monthly spend of ₹3,000, so every customer in the population spends exactly ₹3,000 per month.”


**1. What is wrong or too strong about this conclusion?**

The manager assumes that every customer spends exactly ₹3,000 per month based on the sample average. This is incorrect because individual customers have different spending habits, and the sample average does not represent every customer's spending.

**2. Does a sample mean have to equal the population mean? Explain.**

No, the sample mean does not have to equal the population mean. The selected sample may have different spending patterns from the overall population, causing the two averages to differ.

**3. What information about the sampling process would help you judge whether the sample is useful?**

I would check whether the 10 customers were selected randomly, whether different customer groups are represented, and whether the sample is large and representative enough for the intended analysis.

**4. Rewrite the manager's statement more carefully.**

The selected customers have an average monthly spend of ₹3,000. This sample average may help us estimate the average spending of the full customer population, but it does not mean every customer spends exactly ₹3,000 per month.
